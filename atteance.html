🐝 TextBee SMS Gateway Setup

Connect your Android phone to dispatch automated absent SMS directly from your SIM card

📥 Download TextBee App 🌐 TextBee Dashboard
📋 Step-by-Step Procedure to Get Your API Key & Connect Your Device:
  1. Download & Install App: Open textbee.dev on your Android phone, download the APK, install it, and grant SMS and background/battery permissions.
  2. Create Account: Sign up or log in at textbee.dev/dashboard or directly inside the mobile app.
  3. Add & Connect Device: Open the TextBee app on your phone, sign in with the same account, and tap to register your phone. The app will generate and show your unique Device ID.
  4. Get Your API Key: In your TextBee web dashboard or app profile, copy your personal API Key.
  5. Set Your API Key & Device ID: Enter your API Key and Device ID in the fields below, then click "💾 SAVE PROFILE CHANGES".
⚠️ Notice: Please set your own API Key and Device ID so that all automated absent messages and reminders are dispatched directly from your device and SIM card!
